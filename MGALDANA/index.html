Our Services

Dental Care for Every Smile

Learn more about the dental services offered by M.G. Aldana Dental Clinic. Contact your preferred branch for inquiries, assessment, and service availability.

01

General Dentistry

General dental care focused on maintaining healthy teeth and gums.

English

General dentistry focuses on routine oral care, prevention, assessment, and treatment of common dental concerns based on the dentist's evaluation.

Tagalog

Pangkalahatang pangangalaga sa ngipin at gilagid. Kabilang dito ang regular na pagsusuri at paggamot sa mga karaniwang problema sa ngipin depende sa assessment ng dentista.

Ask About This Service
02

Orthodontics

Dental care focused on improving tooth alignment and bite.

English

Orthodontic care focuses on correcting the position and alignment of the teeth and bite. Treatment options are recommended after proper dental and orthodontic assessment.

Tagalog

Ang orthodontics ay para sa pag-aayos ng alignment o pagkakapantay ng mga ngipin at kagat. Ang angkop na treatment ay malalaman pagkatapos ng pagsusuri ng dentista.

Ask About This Service
03

Cosmetic Dentistry

Dental treatments focused on improving the appearance of your smile.

English

Cosmetic dentistry focuses on improving the appearance of the teeth and smile. The appropriate treatment depends on your dental condition, concerns, and professional assessment.

Tagalog

Ang cosmetic dentistry ay nakatuon sa pagpapaganda ng itsura ng ngipin at ngiti. Ang treatment na maaaring gawin ay depende sa kondisyon at assessment ng dentista.

Ask About This Service
04

Oral Surgery

Surgical dental care for conditions requiring procedures beyond routine treatment.

English

Oral surgery involves dental procedures for conditions that may require surgical treatment. A professional dental assessment is needed to determine the appropriate procedure.

Tagalog

Ang oral surgery ay para sa mga dental condition na maaaring mangailangan ng surgical procedure. Kailangan muna ng pagsusuri ng dentista upang malaman kung anong treatment ang naaangkop.

Ask About This Service
05

Dental Implant

A tooth replacement option for suitable patients with missing teeth.

English

Dental implants are a treatment option for replacing missing teeth. Suitability and the recommended treatment plan are determined through dental examination and professional assessment.

Tagalog

Ang dental implant ay isang option para mapalitan ang nawawalang ngipin. Kailangan muna ng dental examination upang malaman kung ang implant ay angkop para sa pasyente.

Ask About This Service
06

TMD Pain Management

Assessment and management of concerns involving the jaw joint and related discomfort.

English

TMD pain management focuses on evaluating and managing discomfort or other concerns involving the temporomandibular joint and surrounding structures.

Tagalog

Ang TMD pain management ay para sa pagsusuri at management ng pananakit o discomfort na may kaugnayan sa jaw joint o kasukasuan ng panga.
